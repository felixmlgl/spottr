Benchmark of `deck/v2/index.html` (branch `claude/pitch-deck-v2-224a3a`) against YC / seed-investor guidance, 19 early decks (8 YC in `02`, 11 non-YC in `03`) and the company comparables in `04`. Analysis only; nothing in the deck was changed.

**Inputs** (all in this folder):
- `00_our_deck.md`: verbatim inventory of all 23 pages
- `01_yc_guidance.md`: 113 rules (R01–R113) from 19 opened sources
- `02_yc_decks.md`: Airbnb, Coinbase, Dropbox, GitLab, Replit, Amplitude (seed / Demo Day) + Mixpanel B, Brex B (contrast)
- `03_unicorn_decks.md`: Uber, Intercom, Buffer, Foursquare, YouTube, Mint, Tinder (seed / first round) + Facebook, Canva, LinkedIn B, Moz (low weight)

- `05_verification.md`: independent check of the draft of this file. All of its corrections are applied below.

**How to read references.** "R##" = rule ID in `01_yc_guidance.md`. Deck names point to `02`/`03`, comparables to `04`. Each row carries one source link; full source tables are in 01–04. Quotes from 01's non-YC-Library pages and from 04 came through a summarising fetch tool: re-check them on the live page before reusing any of them publicly.

**Scope rules applied.** Only things that need fixing are listed. Known TODOs from `deck/v2/README.md` appear only where the benchmark changes their priority (tagged **known TODO**). No fix asks the deck to claim more than CLAIMS.md or the live product backs; where a reference pattern needs something we don't have, the fix says what we need first. Example rewrites are suggestions; placeholders in [brackets] must be filled from real data.

**Counts:** 47 fixes: **24 × P0**, **14 × P1**, **9 × P2**. IDs are kept stable against `05_verification.md`; NU5, NS8 and DE3 were merged into NS4, YA3 and OC8.

## 1. Top 10 fixes (impact on a YC partner reading in under 3 minutes)

DocSend: investors spend "just under 3 minutes" on a deck before deciding on a meeting and give up on failed decks at 2:13 (S17, `01` §3). In that time a reader sees headlines and big numbers, not footers. Ranked accordingly:

1. **OC1 — Back or reword the traction headline.** "a San Francisco gym chain signed on" / "8 gyms onboarding from November 1" is not in CLAIMS.md. The register says "1 large chain in pilot talks… no pilot agreed, no LOI, no revenue", and its only "8" is "8 replied" to cold emails. Get it in writing or say exactly what exists. One wrong claim here sinks every other slide.
2. **NS2 — Move traction from slide 9 to slide 3–4** (only after OC1). It is the strongest fact in the deck and sits behind eight slides. "Don't bury the good stuff" (R31); speedrun ranks traction "number one, two, and three" (R106).
3. **OC8 + OC15 + OC4 — Make every headline and visual true without its footer.**
   - Slide 8 says "Plug in one box. Use the cameras already on the walls." The box and live ingestion are "in development", and the "Real output" map next to it comes from two phones filmed in a lounge, "not a gym" (`world-service/docs/REPORT.md`).
   - The hook "Every gym already films every workout." contradicts slide 10's "only half of all gyms have cameras".
4. **OC3 + OC5 + HC4 — Drop "first" and "neither"; add the real competitors.** VPixel (Agmis) launched in 2019 and today markets "existing gym cameras" (`04`). Our own setup needs a new box, so "We need neither" fails against our own A4 table.
5. **YA1 — Write the YC answers and video first.** YC's application has no deck field (R93, inferred). It reads the written description and a 1-minute founders-only video (R94–R98). With 20 days to 2026-11-02, the deck's P0s matter most for the a16z/speedrun cut.
6. **NU1 — Lead the market slide with a bottom-up US number, one definition.** The $2.2B headline rests on an unsourced ~245K global gym count. CLAIMS.md says the headline leads with the US chain SAM (R58 bottom-up; R60 realistic beats big).
7. **NS7 — Put amount, runway and milestones on the ask slide** (**known TODO**, raised to P0). Today it shows "$300k" plus three soft asks. Intercom's and Airbnb's ask slides tie money to a dated milestone (R76).
8. **NS1 — Cut to 10–12 main slides, plus a 7-slide cut for speedrun.** 15 + 8 pages sits at speedrun's 15-slide ceiling and double its preferred 7 (R03). It is also above Demo Day's 5–7 (R02), DocSend's 10 (R04) and the seed median of 13 (`02`, `03`).
9. **HC2 + OC11 — Fix the team slide** (**known TODO**, raised to P0). Full names, who built what, technical co-founder, how long the founders have known each other (R71), and education stated exactly. speedrun reviewers check LinkedIn (R105, R107).
10. **NU2 + NU3 — Justify $1,500 per location per month.** It is about 19× the $79 per-location price of gym software (Mindbody, Glofox add-on) and the deck shows no operator ROI. Label it as an untested hypothesis, show break-even math, and test it with the chain.

Just outside the top 10: **DE2.** The hero replay frame reads "#2 squat x0" under "For members: every rep, counted". It is a one-hour fix, so do it anyway.
