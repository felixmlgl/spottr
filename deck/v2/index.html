Problem · two sides, one blind spot

Members won't log their workouts. Gyms can't see their floor.

Members
Tracking is tedious.
  • Nobody wants a notebook or notes app between sets
  • Logging breaks the flow of every workout
  • Wearables track strain, not sets and reps
Gyms
They're flying blind.
  • No granular data on how the floor is used
  • Zones, peak times, popular equipment: all guesswork
  • Members drift away unseen: average retention is 66.4%

A1 · Privacy and data security

We track body pose, not faces. Here is what the build does today.

Available today
Today No face recognition. Sets and reps come from body keypoints over time.
Today Bystanders pixelated head to toe in the member replay.
Today Blurred snapshots only: Gemini gets one small image per set, lifter's face pixelated, others fully pixelated.
Today Operators see aggregates: counts, trends, zones; no photos or video.
Today AI recaps from text (exercise names, reps), not video.
In development
Building All processing on the Spottr box; goal: raw video never leaves the gym
Building Member opt-in and opt-out
Building Retention windows with automatic deletion
Building Self-serve export and deletion
Known limits
Blur only covers people the pose model detects; the replay blurs while drawing, the file itself is not blurred.
