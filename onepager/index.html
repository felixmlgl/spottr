The problem

Why gyms lose track

  • Members don't track consistently

    Logging every set in an app is a chore, so most people skip it. Progress stays invisible.

  • Gyms can't see their own floor

    Operators know who walked in. They don't know what members actually did, or which equipment gets used.

  • Members drop out

    People who can't see progress lose motivation and quietly stop coming.

How it works

Camera in, workout log out

  2. 01

    Camera

    Spottr reads video from cameras the gym already has.

  3. 02

    Spottr recognizes the exercise and counts reps

    A vision model follows body keypoints, not faces, then counts reps, groups them into sets and labels the exercise.

  4. 03

    Workout logged automatically

    Each member's sets, reps and progress appear in their app. No taps needed.

Spottr tracks body pose, not faces, and does not do face recognition. Today it runs on recorded clips. We're building toward running on a device inside the gym, so video would stay on site. [TODO: confirm with team]

Who it's for

Built for the gym and the people in it

For gyms

  • See what happens on the floor

    Know which exercises members do and how often, from data instead of guesses.

  • Offer a premium experience

    Give members automatic tracking and visible progress, without selling them a gadget.

  • Keep members coming back

    Members who see progress have a reason to return. Works with the cameras you already have.

For members

  • Just train

    Your sets and reps are logged without you touching your phone.

  • See your progress

    Review past workouts, replay a set with a skeleton overlay, and track progress over time.

  • Know what's recovered

    A muscle map shows which muscle groups are ready to train again.
